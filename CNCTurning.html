CNC Turning

Turning involves rotation of the workpiece while the cutting tool moves in a linear motion. This results in a cylindrical shape. Contrary to a mill, a lathe operates by fixturing cylindrical stock, called a rod, into a rotating chuck jaw on the machine. As this chuck rotates at a high RPM, so does the stock, and a fixed-orientation tool then translates along and across the stock to cut part features.

For cylindrical parts such as pins, shafts, and spacers, or parts with general rotational symmetry, 'turning' them on a lathe is usually the simplest and most cost effective choice. The ability to program and automate machining operations makes CNC turning suitable for both prototype and production quantities of parts.

Advantages of CNC Turning

CNC turning offers several advantages over conventional manual turning processes. These advantages make CNC turning a preferred choice for precision machining and high-volume production in various industries.

High Precision

It can achieve extremely high levels of precision and tight tolerances, often within micrometers. This precision is essential for industries that require accurate and consistent parts, such as aerospace, medical, and electronics.

Multi-Axis Machining

Many CNC turning machines offer multi-axis capabilities, allowing for simultaneous machining on different sides of the part. This enables the creation of complex features without the need for multiple setups.

Faster Production

CNC turning's automation, optimization, precision, and multitasking capabilities, contribute to faster production rates. This speed advantage is particularly valuable for large production runs.

Consistency & Repeatability

Once a CNC program is developed and tested, the machining process can be repeated identically for each part. This ensures consistent quality across a production run and minimizes variability.

Swiss Turning

Star Engineering Works swiss turning is specialized in machining small-diameter parts with intricate geometries and high precision. It excels at producing long, slender parts with tight tolerances. The precision of Swiss turning makes it ideal for industries that require high-precision parts, such as medical, aerospace, and electronics.

CNC Milling

CNC milling is utilized to craft intricate prismatic forms and flat surfaces, catering to a diverse range of commercial and industrial products. Multi-axis CNC machines possess the ability to achieve their precision and versatility without relying on fixed tooling. Their design allows them to adapt easily to various tasks and shapes, resulting in high flexibility.

EDM Services

EDM(Electrical Discharge Machining) is a specialized machining process that uses electrical discharges or sparks to remove material from a workpiece. EDM is a versatile method that addresses challenges posed by various materials, shapes, and precision requirements in industries ranging from manufacturing to medical devices and beyond.

Surface Finishing

Surface finishing involves various manufacturing processes designed to enhance a product's appearance, durability, and performance. Common methods include polishing, painting, sandblasting, plating, powder coating, brushing, anodizing, and heat treatment, among others. These techniques improve visual aesthetics, protect surfaces from wear and corrosion.
