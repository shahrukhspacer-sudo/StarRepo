CNC Milling

In CNC milling, a computer program (often generated from a CAD design) guides the movement of the cutting tools along multiple axes (usually three or more) to achieve the desired shape and dimensions. The cutting tools may include end mills, drills, and other specialized tools, and they remove material in a subtractive manner, gradually shaping the workpiece into the desired form.

CNC milling offers high levels of accuracy, repeatability, and versatility, making it suitable for producing components with intricate geometries, tight tolerances, and fine surface finishes. It's widely used across industries such as manufacturing, aerospace, automotive, electronics, and more, for applications ranging from prototypes and one-off parts to large-scale production runs.

Understanding Milling Machine Complexity

Simpler parts made for general use might not need very exact measurements or a super smooth surface. But if the design is intricate, it will usually need more precise milling. Milling machines can have 3, 4, or 5 axes.

3-axis machines are common and can cut up and down (Z-axis) and sideways (X and Y). However, they can't easily make certain shapes like undercuts. These need a more special kind of machine. The 4-axis machine is fancier. It can also spin like a lathe.

5-axis milling machines include rotation in both the X and Y-axis. They are the most complete milling machines available and are implemented for highly complex designs such as medical devices and implants for the medical industry, impellers and aerospace structures.

EDM Services

EDM(Electrical Discharge Machining) is a specialized machining process that uses electrical discharges or sparks to remove material from a workpiece. EDM is a versatile method that addresses challenges posed by various materials, shapes, and precision requirements in industries ranging from manufacturing to medical devices and beyond.

Surface Finishing

Surface finishing involves various manufacturing processes designed to enhance a product's appearance, durability, and performance. Common methods include polishing, painting, sandblasting, plating, powder coating, brushing, anodizing, and heat treatment, among others. These techniques improve visual aesthetics, protect surfaces from wear and corrosion, and can also enhance specific mechanical properties depending on the application.
